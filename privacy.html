Privacy Note

Last updated 9 November 2026

This website keeps nothing in your browser and doesn't know who you are. We only receive your details when you choose to send them to us.

Who we are

Paradox Arts is a brand strategy and design studio in Lagos, Nigeria. For any question about your information, email hq.paradoxarts@gmail.com.

What this website collects

  • No cookies, no stored data. The site sets no cookies and saves nothing on your device.
  • Visit statistics. We use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to count visits, the case studies people open and clicks on our contact links. It uses no cookies and doesn't store your IP address or anything else that identifies you. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, your visit isn't counted.

When you contact us

The “Start a project” form doesn't send anything by itself. Pressing “Send via WhatsApp” opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press send there. We then receive what you chose to send: usually your name, company, email or WhatsApp number, what you need, your timeline, a link and your message. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

We use these details only to reply to you and, if we work together, to deliver the project. We don't sell them or pass them to anyone for their own marketing.

Who else handles it

WhatsApp (Meta) and Gmail (Google) carry the messages you send us, Umami provides the visit statistics, and our website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. Some of these services store data outside Nigeria, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry for as long as we need it to reply and follow up, and no longer than 12 months if we don't go on to work together. Project records are kept for as long as the law and our accounts require.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or object to how we use it. Email hq.paradoxarts@gmail.com and we'll respond as soon as we can. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes

If this note changes, the date at the top changes with it.
