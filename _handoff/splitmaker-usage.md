# Splitmaker: where it goes and how it sits

Use sparingly: one Splitmaker per layout, never as decoration.

## Rules
1. **Splitmaker stands alone on the main background.** No tile, panel, glass border or frame around it, ever.
2. **Light surfaces only for now.** On cream (and white, lavender grey) the face, seam and outlines stay clearly visible, which meets the guide's 3:1 contrast target for non-text graphics. On navy and orange, the navy body and legs disappear, so Splitmaker is left out of dark layouts until a dark-surface version of the artwork exists (the guide's Midnight Navy and Burnt Orange examples).
3. **Never on busy imagery.** Never mirror or recolour it. The orange seam keeps its direction.
4. **Small-screen check:** if the face and seam are not clear at half size, use a larger pose or leave it out.
5. **Alt text:** decorative uses carry empty alt text. Meaningful uses name the pose.

## Pose names (from the guide)
Thinking, Explaining, Pointing, Idea, Planning, Celebrating, Learning, Moving forward. Ours so far: hand on chin = Thinking, open hand = Explaining, telescope = Looking ahead, leaning on pencil = the default standing pose, whiteboard = Planning.

## Assets
The images in `mascot/` have clean transparent edges, cut from your pose sheets. The sheets are small, so they are soft at large sizes. For full-size use and for dark surfaces, export transparent poses from your source files and swap them in.
